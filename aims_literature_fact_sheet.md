# Fact sheet for the aims rewrite (verified 2026-10-27)

Big picture
- CMD (coronary microvascular dysfunction) causes angina with open arteries (INOCA) and is implicated in HFpEF, HCM, obstructive CAD, non-ischemic cardiomyopathies, Takotsubo; no specific treatment (Del Buono et al., JACC 2021;78:1352-1371).
- PROMIS-HFpEF: 151/202 (75%) HFpEF patients had CMD (CFR < 2.5) (Shah et al., EHJ 2018;39:3439-3450).
- Angina: ~112 M people; up to 70% of patients undergoing invasive angiography have no obstructive CAD; INOCA outcomes comparable to obstructive CAD; diagnosis by invasive coronary function testing or noninvasive flow reserve (Kunadian et al., EHJ 2020). US registry: 37.6% of 398,978 elective caths had obstructive CAD (Patel, NEJM 2010).
- Current tests judge CMD by CFR (<2.0 abnormal) and microvascular resistance; myocardial blood volume (fraction of tissue that is blood) is an "unexplored feature of microvascular integrity"; MCE is the gold standard for MBV but not routine (Vink et al., Front Cardiovasc Med 2021;8:730810, MICORDIS).
- Contraction expels intramyocardial blood: total IMBV 8.6 ml/100 g in diastolic arrest vs 5.0 in systolic arrest (-42%) in rat (Judd & Levy, Circ Res 1991;68:217-225).
- Mechanisms of muscle-vessel cross-talk: vascular waterfall, intramyocardial pump, varying elastance (Westerhof et al., Physiol Rev 2006); Spaan 1981 pump.
- Regional volume change in vivo: 4.1/6.8/10.3% (subepi/mid/subendo) in dogs; inflow predicts only ~1% (Ashikaga 2008). Sheep: J_ES = 0.85 (15%), up to 25% at endocardium; infarct loses it (Avazmohammadi 2020).
- Review (Brown, Liu, Ennis, Marsden, arXiv 2509.07971, 2025): most models treat myocardium as essentially incompressible; reported volume changes range from <2% (MRI) to ~25% (endocardium, sonomicrometry); "degree and mechanisms incompletely understood"; more research needed to determine how to model it.
- Incompressible vs compressible FE model: peak active stress 182.8 vs 107.4 kPa (Liu, Soares et al., Sci Rep 2021).
- Clinical ratio: CMR MVs/MVd 0.88 normal vs 1.03 HCM/amyloid, 0.97 HFpEF (Ryu 2021); ES > ED in all groups on CMR (Mazurkiewicz 2017); guideline echo models assume constant myocardial volume (Kumar 2021); literature median ES/ED 0.93 from strains (Bonnemains 2019).
- IMBV by RBC-SPECT: 0.15 ED, 0.11 ES, change 23.9% in dogs (Mohy-ud-Din 2018); human pilot (Yousefi 2023).
- Poromechanical models with coronary flow exist as forward simulations (Cookson 2012; Lee 2016; Chapelle; Di Gregorio; Barnafi); none identified against in vivo volume+flow+pressure data.
